MCHOSE V9 Pro - WebHID OTA Flasher

Self-contained. Protocol matched verbatim to the official MCHOSE PC OTA tool (class S$). Chrome/Edge only (requires WebHID).

READ BEFORE FLASHING

  • Power on the V9 Pro headset and keep it within 2.4G range of the dongle. ENTER UPDATE for the headset only succeeds if the headset is powered, paired, and in range -- the dongle relays the firmware over 2.4G. If READY never arrives, this is the usual cause.
  • If unsure, click DIAGNOSTIC first. It connects, sends ENTER UPDATE, and for 15s dumps every input report and readable feature report. Paste that output back if it still fails.
  • Flash headset_noop.bin FIRST. It is byte-identical to stock and proves the whole flash path (enter / stream / verify) works on your unit before you change anything.
  • Keep your original stock firmware (the .bin you downloaded from MCHOSE) somewhere safe. It is your recovery image.
  • Do NOT unplug the dongle/headset, sleep the PC, or close this tab during a flash.
  • Keep this browser tab in the FOREGROUND for the entire flash. Do not switch windows, minimize, or open another tab. Browsers throttle background tabs, which freezes the firmware stream; the headset idle-times-out after ~10s without data and aborts. A screen wake lock is requested automatically, and a red banner will appear if focus is lost.
  • Flash HEADSET firmware only here. Do not feed it a dongle image.
  • This is your own hardware. You accept the risk of bricking it.
